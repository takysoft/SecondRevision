# Height maps for the New Vision textures

The relief the renderer's `Geometry` setting draws the New Vision textures with (`POM`, `DisplacementMapping`):

- `NewVision/<Package>.heightmap` - the maps of one New Vision texture package, with their depths

Copy them beside the New Vision texture packages: `Deus Ex\Revision\NewVision\Textures` for Revision.

To make height maps for your own textures, see `MAKING-HEIGHTMAPS.md`.

## Origin and terms

The maps are AI generated from the New Vision textures by Dave Watts, with textures by David Guttridge, Thijs Hagens
and Helder Pinto (https://www.moddb.com/mods/new-vision). New Vision's textures may be used in Deus Ex mods, with
credit to New Vision. These maps are derived from them, so they are for use with Deus Ex and its mods only, under
the same terms. They are not covered by the zlib licence of the renderer's code.
